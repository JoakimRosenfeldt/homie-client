Round 1 · interactive prototype

Nabo — find a room, and the people in it

Two halves of one app. Explore is a conventional, filter-heavy property search with a map — you are looking for a place. Applicants is the flipped side — as the person renting out the room, you swipe through the profiles of people who applied, and chat with matches. The tab bar keeps the two modes clearly separated so it never reads as a dating app.

Direction: soft daylight — pale blue-grey ground, generous rounded cards, terracotta as the single accent, Figtree throughout. Assumptions: Copenhagen market, DKK, students + young professionals, owner-swipes-applicant model, map as a toggle from the list. Everything below is live — tap tabs, filters, listing cards, swipe or drag the applicant cards, open a match and send a message. Photos are labelled placeholders.

How the two modes stay apart

Explore and Map are cool, dense, property-first — image-led cards, hard numbers, no faces. Applicants is warm and single-focus — one person at a time, one big name, and language borrowed from hiring ("shortlist") rather than dating.

Try

Tap the terracotta filter button · open any listing and apply · drag an applicant card left or right · shortlist Amir to trigger a match · open a match and send a message · under You, tap the terracotta card to run the profile builder.

Open questions

Should the seeker also swipe (mutual match), or only apply? Do existing flatmates get a vote on a shortlist? And is the applicant queue per-listing or per-household?
